Banking, Private Equity & Financial Services

Led by Ashmita Ray Sarkar

Our historic core practice. Banking and financial services has always been IQ’s core business, and our associates have worked in the sector themselves.

We identify and assess board members, CEOs and senior functional leaders, and the teams behind them. Our network of senior management runs across banks, private equity and financial institutions, and several managers we placed have gone on to the C‑suite.

Areas

  • Investment banking
  • Asset and wealth management
  • Private equity
  • Retail and consumer banking
  • Risk management

Legal

Led by Shirin Gulrajani Talwar

The legal function keeps changing. Companies are bringing more legal capability in-house and relying on their legal teams to stay ahead of compliance and governance.

We recruit from in-house counsel to law-firm associates, senior partners and whole practice groups, with a working understanding of what makes a legal hire succeed.

Areas

  • In-house counsel
  • Law-firm associates
  • Senior partners
  • Practice groups

Media, Advertising & Hospitality

Led by Annu Paul

We hire mid- to senior-level leaders for media owners, publishers and advertising agencies, with a focus on senior management, account planning, account management and digital strategy for agencies serving FMCG and consumer brands.

In hospitality, our consultants have long experience hiring for hotels and work across regions to find the right fit.

Areas

  • Agency leadership
  • Account planning and management
  • Digital strategy
  • Publishing
  • Hotels and hospitality
