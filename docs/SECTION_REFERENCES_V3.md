## 1. Masthead hero (typographic, no photo yet)

The portrait files are too small for a hero, so the v3 hero launches **without a photo** (see V3_PLAN D3).

| Reference | Observed | Take for IQ |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Tiger Global** (`tigerglobal.png`) | A flat grey panel. The hero is a **two-sentence paragraph** with key phrases in bold ("research-driven", "long-term approach", "identify and invest"), plus "more than 25 years of experience". No image, no CTA clutter. | **Closest match to "institution".** The statement *is* the hero. IQ: the headline stays, and the subheadline becomes a confident paragraph with 2–3 bold phrases (*partner-led*, *30+ years*). |
| **Every** (`every.png`) | A centred serif wordmark masthead, a thin rule, then a magazine grid. | A masthead rule and dateline row (*Mumbai · Oslo · 30+ years*) under the nav. |
| **C. Hoare & Co.** (`../v3-research/hoares.png`) | A flat deep-colour panel with serif caps. | Flat plum or paper panel. The photo half is added when office photos arrive (the H1 split). |

**Pattern:** a paper or flat plum panel, wordmark and text nav, a hairline, the headline at about 64px, a subheadline paragraph with bold key phrases, the dateline row and one CTA. About 70–80vh. An empty half is reserved for the future photo, so the layout doesn't change when it arrives.

## 2. Note under the hero (current statement, later the founder's note)

| Reference | Observed | Take |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Basecamp** (PageProofer teardown) | "A signed founder letter", "a page whose argument is mostly words, real people and real product." | Letter format: a reading measure of about 62 characters, set like correspondence. A signature and byline **only when Ashmita supplies her words**. |
| **Are.na** (`arena.png`) | Text-led. A precise, living fact in bold ("For the last **15 years and 89 days**…"). | One precise fact in the note, e.g. *"30+ years"* or the number of mandates, once confirmed. |
| **Tiger Global** | Bold key phrases inside prose. | The same device as the hero, used sparingly. |

**Pattern:** a margin label in columns 1–3 (*How we work* now; *A note from our founder* later), prose in columns 4–9, and a rule above. For now it carries the approved statement ("Every mandate is led by a partner…"), **without a byline**: a sentence we wrote can't be signed with her name until she approves it.

## What these references have in common

1. **Text does the persuading.** Tiger Global, Are.na and Basecamp put sentences, facts and lists first, with imagery secondary or absent.
2. **One structure repeated:** label on the left, content on the right (Pentagram, Are.na, Stripe Press). That's the cure for the slide rhythm.
3. **Uniform, plain portraits** (Pentagram) read as partners. Styled cards read as product.
4. **Ledger and index formats** (Basecamp, Stripe Press) make a track record feel factual.
5. **Avoid what even good references do:** pill toggles (Sequoia), dark-mode heroes (Are.na and Basecamp now render dark) and oversized spaced-caps titles (Sequoia). They conflict with the paper-and-ink direction.
