Method

Five steps, one partner throughout.

  1. Understand your business

    Strategic priorities, the goals of the mandate, and your culture.

  2. Shortlist candidates

    Proprietary databases, market mapping and direct outreach.

  3. Identify the match

    Competency evaluation and 360° reference checks.

  4. Hire & onboard

    Offer negotiation, closing and transition support.

  5. Monitor & iterate

    Post-placement check-ins, so the hire lasts.
